Sunrise and Sunset Dashboard

The Sunrise and Sunset Dashboard is a neat web app that helps you find out when the sun rises and sets anywhere in the world. You can choose from popular cities like Los Angeles, New York, London, Tokyo, and Moscow, use your current location, or enter custom latitude and longitude if you're up for it. It shows you important details like sunrise time, sunset time, solar noon, and how long the day lasts for both today and tomorrow. What's cool is that you can customize what information you see with simple filters, making the whole experience interactive and user-friendly.
